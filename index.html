About the Company

Labour contracting, run on systems and records

Mahendra Facility Services is a Pune-based labour contractor supplying manpower on contract for defined job works. The workers we deploy are experienced in their respective trades, familiar with machine and equipment handling, and work under our own site supervisors.

Our clientele spans manufacturing, casting, forging, painting, powder-coating and electrical units, along with automotive-parts manufacturers. Every worker is our direct employee — wages, provident fund, ESIC and welfare-fund obligations are met by us and documented month on month.

  • Direct-employee model — statutory liability stays with us, not you
  • Computerized attendance, wage registers and compliance records
  • Qualified supervisors on site; absentee cover and replacements managed daily
  • Manpower scales up or down with your production cycles
